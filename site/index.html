A money planner for first-time investors. It shows what a bad year would cost you, and the news that explains why.

Most apps only show the upside.

Question one

What should I invest in, for my goals?

A personal, diversified plan from real Indian and US stocks, with a safe portion that grows as a goal gets closer.

Question two

How much could I lose?

A bad year, the average of the worst 5%, and a repeat of the Covid crash or 2008, all in rupees, from 3,000 simulated years.

Question three

My friend said buy X. Should I?

A verdict for you, with the news red flags an AI found in the headlines, and safer stocks that fit better.

Try it in a minute.

  1. Download Vardhan.zip and unzip it anywhere.
  2. Double-click Vardhan.exe. If Windows warns you, click More info → Run anyway. It isn't code-signed, which costs money.
  3. Click Try the demo to meet Aarav, 21, saving for a Master's and a house, with six months of investments already in.
  4. Optional: install Ollama, run ollama pull llama3.2:3b, and Vani answers on your own laptop.

An education project, not investment advice. In India, personalised advice needs SEBI registration. On macOS or Linux, build from source with make.
